Are product information and prices verified?

No. Product names, images, prices, discounts, ratings, specifications, and availability are illustrative sample data.

Can I place a real order or make a payment?

No. This is a front-end demonstration. The checkout does not contact a payment provider or fulfilment service.

Does the support form send an email?

No. A valid submission is saved locally in the current browser to demonstrate form behavior.
